# Battalion Cards Version 1.5 (2025)

**🆕 MAJOR CHANGES FROM v1.4:** Fewer troops (40 vs 58), more captains (12 vs 7), smaller faster deck!

This document outlines the complete card composition for Battalion v1.5, with rebalanced card quantities based on 15,000 games of simulation data.

The deck includes cards from the Playbook (Commanders and Mages), the Trick Book, and the Main Deck (Troops, Captains, Contracts, and Towers).

## Card Overview

**Total:** 114 cards + Tower cards (3 per player)
- Playbook Cards: 18 (10 Commanders + 8 Mages)
- Trick Book: 26
- Main Deck: 76 (**40 Troops** + 12 Contracts + **12 Captains** + 12 Towers)

### Changes from v1.4:
- **Troops: 58 → 40** (4 of each rank instead of 6)
- **Captains: 7 → 12** (71% increase!)
- **Total Main Deck: 89 → 76** (13 cards smaller, faster games)

## Main Deck Cards

### Troops (Total: 40) 🆕 REDUCED FROM 58

**Why fewer troops?** Simulation data showed 65% troops = too easy to build massive armies. Turtle hoarded troops and won without leaders. New ratio: 52% troops = strategic constraint.

#### White/Red Troops (20 total)
- 002 - White/Red 1 **x4** (was x6)
- 003 - White/Red 2 **x4** (was x6)
- 004 - White/Red 3 **x4** (was x6)
- 005 - White/Red 4 **x4** (was x6)
- 006 - White/Red 5 **x4** (was x6)

#### Black Troops (20 total)
- 007 - Black 1 **x4** (was x6)
- 008 - Black 2 **x4** (was x6)
- 009 - Black 3 **x4** (was x6)
- 010 - Black 4 **x4** (was x6)
- 011 - Black 5 **x4** (was x6)

*Note: White troops changed to White/Red designation in Deck v1.0*

**Impact of fewer troops:**
- Troops are now valuable resources (not abundant)
- Harder to build 5-troop armies without commitment
- Forces strategic placement decisions
- Faster games (smaller deck cycles quicker)

### Captains (Total: 12) 🆕 INCREASED FROM 7

**Why more captains?** Only 8% of deck = rarely drawn naturally. 99% of leaders were bought/contracted. New ratio: 16% = captains actually drawable!

#### 015 - Captain Art v1 **x6** (was x3)
*Visual variant featuring team member photo (Nathan)*

#### 016 - Captain Art v2 **x6** (was x4)
*Visual variant featuring team member photo (Luke)*

- Captains have unit value of 1 and attack power of 1
- May attack on the turn they are played
- Claim an army and select a trick to use with it
- **🆕 v1.5:** With leaders now REQUIRED to claim, captains are essential! Drawing one from the deck is now a viable path to claiming armies.

**Impact of more captains:**
- Natural claiming is viable (don't HAVE to buy/contract)
- More flexible strategy options
- Reduces early game deadlock (stuck with unclaimed armies)
- Balances the "leaders required" rule

### Contract Cards (Total: 12) ✅ UNCHANGED QUANTITY, BUT NOW COST 2 INFLUENCE

#### 014 - Contract x12
- Can be discarded during your action step to select a Commander or Mage from the playbook
- **🆕 v1.5:** Now costs 2 influence (was 3 in v1.4, was 1 in v1.3)
- Does not count as an action
- **Trade-off:** Contract (2 inf) = cheap weak leader vs Buy (1 inf + card) = expensive strong leader (+1 attack)

**Why contracts cost 2 now:**
- At 1 influence (v1.3): 98% usage = too spammy
- At 3 influence (v1.4): 4% usage = dead mechanic
- At 2 influence (v1.5): Expected 40-50% usage = balanced choice!
